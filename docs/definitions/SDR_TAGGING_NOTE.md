# For SDRs — three habits that keep your numbers right

1. **Log every call outcome**, especially when you dial from your phone or outside the dialer. Calls with no outcome count only if they ran longer than the conversation threshold.
2. **Book meetings through Alternate CRM** (call outcome *Meeting Scheduled* with the date, or Change Stage). That is what records you as the booker. A meeting booked only in Salesforce or a calendar is invisible here.
3. **Use "Mark Meeting Complete" or "No-show"** after the meeting. Booked but never marked shows up as "overdue to confirm" once the weekly report exists.

**Tags:** when you upload leads, pick the campaign tag from the list Marketing gives you (see CAMPAIGN.md). Don't create new ones.

**If a lead was handed to you:** your Dashboard splits *Assigned to me* into **new**, **inherited** (someone else worked it — read the history first) and **in progress**.
